# Research Blog: Cross-Sectional Return Patterns in Liquid Crypto

Blog-style quantitative research summary.

Sorting the top-50 liquid crypto assets into quintiles by trailing 30-day
return and rebalancing weekly produces a persistent spread between winners
and losers in most years since 2020, with the notable exception of regime
turns, where the strategy suffers sharp reversals. The winner-minus-loser
spread is stronger when computed on volume-weighted returns and weaker for
market-cap-weighted portfolios dominated by BTC and ETH.

The cross-sectional ranking appears to carry information beyond simple time
series momentum: assets in the top quintile on both trailing return and
volume growth outperform assets that rank high on return alone. Turnover is
substantial, and the spread narrows materially after assuming 10 bps per
side in costs, though it remains positive for weekly rebalancing.

The post shares direction and a rough universe definition but not the exact
ranking formula, weighting scheme, or risk controls used in the author's
backtest.
