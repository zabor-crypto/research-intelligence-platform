# Research Report: The State of Crypto Basis and Funding Carry (Q2)

Industry research-report style source.

Perpetual funding rates across major venues have compressed since the spring
deleveraging, but dislocations persist around event windows. Annualized basis
on quarterly futures briefly exceeded 15% during the April rally while
funding on the same assets stayed near flat, an unusually wide gap between
the two carry channels.

Desks harvesting carry report that most of the year's return came from a
handful of episodes where funding stayed above the 95th percentile of its
trailing quarter for more than two days. Entering earlier than that mostly
added noise. Positions held past funding normalization gave back roughly a
third of the captured carry.

The report does not disclose exact entry rules; desks differ on hedge
rebalance cadence (every funding interval vs daily) and on whether to size by
funding z-score or fixed notional. Costs of 5 bps per side are considered
achievable for the liquid pairs.
